RPGGAME - where the sounds come from

ui/, voice/, devmapambience.ogg
    The game's own recordings (supplied with the project's assets). The only recorded human voices the game uses are
    voice/ - the death cries, male and female; hurt sounds are the opening moment of the same recordings.

foley/footstep_*, foley/impact*
    "Impact Sounds" 1.0 by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - Creative Commons Zero (CC0), public domain.

foley/ (beltHandle, book*, chop, cloth*, creak*, door*, drawKnife*, dropLeather, footstep00-09, handle*, knifeSlice*, metal*)
    "RPG Audio" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - Creative Commons Zero (CC0), public domain.

sfx/
    "80 CC0 RPG SFX" by rubberduck (opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx) - Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

swish/
    "Swishes Sound Pack" (opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack) - Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

animal/ (cow_*, cow_field, hens_lay, henhouse, hen_lays, hen_annoyed, rooster_*, pig_*, horse_*, sheep_*, dog_*, cat_*, ducks)
    BigSoundBank by Joseph Sardin (bigsoundbank.com) - Creative Commons Zero (CC0), public domain. Sound numbers 2386, 2383,
    2385, 2382, 0546, 0978, 0282, 0976, 0453, 0283, 0440, 1659, 1658, 1691, 1692, 1693, 1543, 1215, 0863, 0284, 1542, 1541,
    0460, 2343, 2344, 2345, 2348, 2349, 2955, 0682, 0288, 1890, 0494, 1901, 1887, 1881, 0276.

animal/wolf_howls.ogg
    "Wolf howls" (Wikimedia Commons, File:Wolf_howls.ogg) - public domain.

animal/bear_vocal.mp3, animal/bear_roar.mp3
    Yellowstone sound library, NPS & MSU Acoustic Atlas / Jennifer Jerrett (Wikimedia Commons) - public domain (US National Park Service).

animal/coyotes_pack.ogg
    "Pack of coyotes howling" by Rybkovich (Wikimedia Commons, File:Pack_of_coyotes_howling.ogg) - Creative Commons
    Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Credit required; the game cuts it into single calls at load time.

    The game's own recordings (supplied with the project's assets).
