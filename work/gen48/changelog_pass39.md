## Pass 39 - Alpha 0.4.8 "Troll Call" (Hoodie)

The 0.4.8 gamefixes pass: the bug list (encounters placed in plain sight and standing idle, silent arrows, the outdoors unmuffled indoors, the rain's sound, rain and snow together, the Unlit Lantern's door, basement and floors, torches and plants, strewn cobble, rare encounters, the Drunken Duck's darkness, NPC daily tasks and monster camps by people), the changes (world map, journal, enemy levels by distance from Loxford, dungeon detail, skills unlock text, starter kit, shields 2x2, coloured level tags, gold separators), and the additions (monster idle animations, snow and puddles with footprints, fifteen destructible furnishings, ice and river trolls, scorpions, lesser and greater demons). Native engine changed (outdoor-voice muffling in the mixer, rain/snow exclusivity, snow caps and model snow, puddles that grow and dry, footprint decals): rebuilt with `build.bat game`.

New tests and tools: `rpg.world.Gamefixes048Test` (11 checks: version and small changes; the Lantern's lintel and every sealed stair's cellars cut with doors; stones not strewn; rooms lit through; rain and snow never together; encounters unseen; danger by distance from Loxford; the seven new monsters made, figured, the scorpion's pincer holding and sting poisoning; the fifteen furnishings modelled and placed; the journal and the map's markers saved; footprint pictures). Probe `EncounterLifeProbeTest` (every encounter forced: spawn distance, sight, movement). `WorldInspectProbeTest` prints the point's block, a structure's doorways and the cellars' notes. `Terrain.stage` prints watched cells after every generation stage (`RPG_CELL_WATCH="x,y,z;..."`); `RPG_CELLAR_DEBUG` prints each cellar's plan. Gallery: p29 `RPG_REVIEW_ONLY=p34ui` (journal, map, skills), `p34snow` (snow and rain with prints); p24 `furn48`; monster sheets film each look, the scorpion's sting and the greater demon's hover. `EncounterTest`'s highwaymen walk lengthened (they now come in further off).

### Bugs
- Encounters: `Encounters.unseen/spawnable` - every spawn point at least `spawnMin` 32 m off, out of the player's sight (eye to head and knees) unless past `spawnSeen` 95 m, on walkable open ground (no water, no roof, no lot), searched round the asked point then out along its bearing; each cast member likewise. "around" spawns no longer pulled in to 0.7 of the distance.
- Encounter people idle: `Brain.goTo` no longer sends encounter people home by the zone policy (they stood at their spawn when the player was in a realm); `AiSystem.threatFor` keeps an encounter's own fight (`Encounters.fights`: the watch on outlaws); lurkers (`hide`) come out after `patience` 40 s with the player within `patienceReach` 130; `hold` faces the nearest of its party and talks (`chatter`); `plead` kneels (`Npc.kneeling`, Rig's kneel); herds migrate a `migrateStride` 18 m at a time; an encounter the player has left `despawnDistance` behind for `leftBehind` 30 s ends (two left behind used to block the director for good). Director: interval 4, chance 0.12, maxActive 3, minApart 120, quietMinutes 20, realmDensity 0.7 (`work/gen48/encounters_data.py`). Probe: idle 22 -> 10 (the rest wait by design), none within 30 m.
- Arrows: `CombatSystem.impactNoise` (`combat.arrowNoise` 11) where an arrow or thrown thing strikes; the bow's string heard (`combat.bowNoise` 6). `Stealth.hear` places the noise at the impact.
- Muffling: native `ASTRATTO_VOICE_OUTDOOR` - a non-positional bed low-passed (`muffle_`, eased from the listener's enclosure) and lowered while indoors; the ambience bed and the weather's beds carry it.
- Rain's sound: real recordings (`work/gen48/hqsnd/build_rain.py`): light rain in a wood over rain on leaves, a downpour of leaves, NASA's heavy rain and the terrace; `rain_roof.ogg` heard only under a roof; `rpg.audio.RainDrops` scatters single recorded drops (stone, water, leaf, roof, soft) round the listener each frame (`audio.json` weather.drops). Loudness swing of the beds -18 dB (the old close-miked bed -10). Credits in `sounds/CREDITS.txt`.
- Rain and snow together: engine `Weather.update` lets one stop before the other starts, a target of both falls as the greater; `snap` likewise. Sleet is rain with hail.
- Unlit Lantern door (627, 52, 552): `Architecture.doorSteps` treated the cellar stair's head (an opening inside the tower) as a street door and cut steps out across the tower floor, taking the front door's lintel; openings whose way out stays inside the building are skipped. A round tower's door jambs are walled where the rim curves away.
- Basement to nowhere (644, 40, 552): new `rpg.world.shadowveil.Cellars` (a Terrain stage after the dungeons): every stair down that is not a dungeon's mouth (Loxford and Valgard undercrofts, the Drunken Duck's cellar, the museum vault, the Lantern's cellars) gets its door hung (`InnerDoor`) and rooms cut beyond: a vestibule (a flight down of up to five steps when the rock over the vaults is thin), a vaulted hall (pillared when wide), side chambers through arches, a back chamber; turned straight on, left, right, or back beside the stair, whichever has the most cover; only where there is rock enough, no water and nobody else's lot or volume; flood-filled from the door; dressed from `dungeons.json` "cellars" (stores, wine, undercroft, archive), sconces every dozen cells, a store chest at the back. `Kit.stairDown` records `Out.cellars` -> `Architecture.cellars`.
- Repeated floors (630, 70, 552): `programs.tower.levels.upperCycle` - the upper storeys take turns through watch room, storeroom, study, armoury, chamber, library, started by the building's hash.
- Torch and foliage: `Foliage.Patch` "Set alight" with a lit torch; a lit torch carried through growth kindles the plant at its head (`Actor.torchBrushAt`, every 0.35 s); any swing kindles plants along its arc (`Foliage.kindle/reaching`).
- Cobble everywhere (468, 54, 572): `Terrain.boulders` rolled against nine times a biome's share (the stony moor's 0.12 put a cluster on every patch); now the share itself, and a cluster's stones bedded flush in the turf, only its heart standing proud.
- Encounters too rare: see the director above.
- Drunken Duck too dark (324, 40, 994): `Fitout.coverLight` - no floor further than `lightReach` 5 beyond a room light's reach (a hearth counts less, a bedside lamp not at all); a sconce goes on the wall nearest the darkest floor, up to `lightsAdded` 6 a room (`placeHang` "nearU/nearV").
- NPC daily tasks: start spots only under the building's own roof (`AiSystem.scatterStart`: a scholar began the day in the lane behind her church and fell into a neighbour's door-step cut); a way stalled on `ai.stuckFarTries` 5 times is given up for the place itself, unseen, however far. WorldLifeProbe: 7 people flagged (Maelin, Danek, Sella, Alaric, Varek, Elidor, Gwenna) before the last two fixes; not rerun after them.
- Monsters by people: `Monsters.territories` keeps camps `clearance.people` 60 from every NPC's post and home, `road` 22 from the roads.

### Changes
- World map: `HudFragments.WorldMap` rewritten - the map scene's frame grows to the screen (`Hud.frameWidth/frameHeight`, `mapFrameW/H`); wheel zoom about the cursor to `map.zoomMax` 12, drag to pan, click for a destination, right-click to drop or lift a marker (`QuestLog.mapMarks`, saved, at most `map.markersMost` 24); a grid every 200/100/50 m numbered, a compass, a scale bar, the region/realm/coordinates under the cursor, services' icons from `map.iconsFrom` 2.2x; the active quest starred (held to the edge); side panel with place, active quest, markers (fly to, remove) and key; the sea beyond the land. The old legend and the journal's map key (`Panels.Legend`) removed.
- Journal: `rpg.quest.QuestLog` (data `quests.json`: the courier quest, carried warrants as side quests; status colours; requirements met or not; giver, destination, objective, notes; active quest; filter showUnstarted (off) / showUnmet, sort; saved "questLog"). `Panels.Journal` rewritten 868 wide: Quests / Notes / The Law tabs, Main and Side lists, overview with Set Active, settings sheet. The minimap stars the active quest's destination.
- Enemy levels: `rpg.world.shadowveil.Danger` (`shadowveil.json` "danger": origin Loxford, safe 90, far 1000, curve 1.15, low [1, 55], high [10, 110], region offsets) - monster territories take their place's band and a kind too strong for the place is not settled there; ShadowVeil dungeon residents take the band at the mouth by depth and level, the master at the top; encounters' fighting cast mapped into the band.
- Dungeons: `Underground.dress` - patterned floors (crypt flags bordered in brick and moss, cave gravel and cobble with puddles, warren mud, hall gravel at the walls), wall plinths and patches (moss, basalt and granite streaks, timber posts), beams across hall and warren rooms, cave stalactites, sarcophagi in crypt rooms, boulders in caves, barrels, crates, sacks, tables, busts and candle stands against the walls clear of passages and spots. 18 of 18 levels still reached, 18 of 18 shafts whole.
- Skills: the unlock list as a coloured verb tag column and the thing it opens (`Panels.Guide.verbOf`), wrapped lines under the text.
- Starter kit: no pot or bucket. Shields 2 x 2. Gold `Currency.text` with its separator. Level tags (`Interactable.levelTag`, `Style.levelColor`: 3+ below green, within 2 yellow, 3+ above red) on nameplates, the focus prompt, fish and the target frame; `Monster.label` and `Fish.label` carry no level.
- Tuning: 56 keys read from code with defaults added to `tuning.json`. Dead code: `Household.at`, `Panels.Legend`, `HudFragments.legend`.

### Additions
- Monsters: `MonsterKind` ICE_TROLL, RIVER_TROLL, SMALL_SCORPION (3-25), LARGE_SCORPION (26-50), LESSER_DEMON, GREATER_DEMON; TROLL titled Mountain troll; `troll()`, `scorpion()`, `demon()`, `variants()`; `Monster.variant` (saved; the greater demon's crested and bearded look at level 100+). `MonsterModels.of(kind, breed, variant)`: `trollKin` (ice: blue-grey, frost, mane, icicle beard; river: lean, long-armed, weed, froggish maw, webbed feet), `scorpion` (plates, eight legs, pincers, five-segment tail and sting), `lesserDemon` (flayed-muscle red, curved bone horns, fanged snarl; tongue and spined looks), `greaterDemon` (red or purple, large bat wings, thick horns, barbed tail), `demonHead`. Humanoid carriage per kind; the greater demon hovers (`hoverFrom/Until`, `MonsterMind` trigger, `monsters.hoverLift` 0.7) on slow hard wing-beats; scorpions drawn on the spider's body with `Gfx.scorpionParts`. Attacks: pincer holds (`Actor.Combat.heldT`, `monsters.pincerHold` 1.3 s) and sting poisons (`poisonT/poisonDps`, `monsters.venomSeconds` 8); the vitals show Poisoned / Held fast. Spawns: `shadowveil.json` monsters by biome (desert, dunes, sandstone; highland, crags; marsh, lakeshore, willowmeadow, marches; volcanic, cinders, scorched), `most` 56; Ashmaw's Throat's residents. Rumours in `Dialogue`.
- Monster idle animations: `Gfx.idleClips` per kind - look about, stretch, scratch, sniff, beat chest, shift weight - every eight-odd seconds when at rest.
- Snow and puddles: `world_gbuffer.frag` snow caps the top edges of open-sky faces as it deepens, puddles grow and dry with wetness; `model_gbuffer.frag` snow on face-up surfaces under the sky (`uSnowCover`); `DecalAtlas` FOOTPRINT and HOOFPRINT; a print's negative tint alpha keeps snow from burying it (`decal.frag`). `rpg.render.Prints` (from `Rig.plant` and `Animals.hooves`) -> `Gfx.prints`: pressed into snow (`weather.snowPrintLife` 1200 s), wet on soaked ground (`weather.wetPrintLife` 100 s), in mud and sand.
- Furnishings: `Furniture.Kind` BARREL, KEG, LECTERN, SPINNING_WHEEL, SCROLL_RACK, THRONE, CRADLE, COAT_RACK, BUTTER_CHURN, GLOBE, TOOL_RACK, MAP_TABLE, DISPLAY_CABINET, FOLDING_SCREEN, SACKS (`Furnishings`), breakable by `Wreckage` as every furnishing is, placed by the interiors' uses and the cellars' dresses.
