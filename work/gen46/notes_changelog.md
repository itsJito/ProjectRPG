## Pass 37 - Alpha 0.4.6 "Weather or Not" (Hoodie)

The 0.4.6 gamefixes pass on ShadowVeil: the bug list (stairs, spinning and stuck NPCs, paintings, the jail's goods and breakouts, graves, water steps, inventory turning, the world border, fish, chandeliers, the law's bodies, foliage pop, ragdolls on ledges, terror), the changes (stations, weapons, accusations, Jail, E to close, weather, foley, debris, region names, shoves), NPC interaction poses, optimisation and the dungeons' groundwork. Native engine changed (pathfinder, chunk mesher, Impulsis contacts, renderer + two new shaders): rebuilt with `build.bat game`.

New tests and tools: `rpg.world.Gamefixes046Test` (10 checks: version; nobody begins within 1 m of another; every station roofed; the margin of sea and the edge's harm; all 23 weathers, Azharad desert, a fortnight of cycles on the hour with only natural weathers and a sandstorm, the console's whiteout; the jail's chest holds an arrested player's things and gives them back, charges read out; no "gaol" anywhere; old headstones; restoreWorld puts a block back; region names and footfall surfaces). Probes `WorldLifeProbeTest` (greedy stops over every home; per person spins - a turn and a half in five seconds without going two metres - blocked and still-on-errand samples, start clusters; `-Prpg.probeTrace=Name,Name` prints position, goal, legs, stalls, nudges and any door's state), `StairPointsProbeTest` (a player-sized walker along any points "x,y,z;..|.."), `StationAuditProbeTest` (stations with no roof over them). Gallery `RPG_REVIEW_WEATHER="x,y,z[,hour];name,name"` (each weather forced and settled, street and high views; no names: all). `rpg.probeTrace` passed by the probe task.

### Bugs
- Stairs at 217 39 913 (Loxford church): the tower volume's flight took the whole row between the tower door and the arch into the nave; `whole()` counted reached cells only, and each side was reached from its own entry. Fitout now records which ways in are openings into the next volume (`Storey.openings`) and `groups()` counts openings no door's floor reaches; a flight or a partition may not raise it (furniture may: a counter parts a hall on purpose). The church tower now has a ladder up and the nave is joined; elsewhere 2 flights became ladders, none lost where a house's two doors were simply parted.
- Spinning (altars, workshops): the Jobs TaskNetwork re-chose a failing method every think (a tailor flipping between "buy missing inputs" at the counter and "tidying the workplace" every 0.7 s). Methods are wrapped (`Jobs.step`): `ai.jobMethodFails` (3) failures, counted at most one per 0.5 s, rest the method `ai.jobMethodRest` 45 s; `tendWorkplace` spots change every `ai.tendDwell` 15 game minutes (was 6). Border knights stand `ai.beatDwell` 12 s at each end of the beat. Probe: 46 people with trouble -> 16.
- Prayer: places were validated at the walker's own height and could stand in a pew; now at the altar's floor and never a seat cell; a place no route reaches is given up (`prayFailX`), a second miss ends the day's prayers; the goal-rest marks the day prayed.
- goTo: "as close as I can get" was true for one call then sought again for two seconds (`nearTX..`: the spot stays reached while the body stays put). A goal more than 12 cells above or below the body's level had no surface and stood "looking for supported ground" for ever: the column's ground is used. A stalled body whose next waypoint is within `ai.nudgeReach` 1.9 m (beside, a step, a drop) takes the step (`ai.nudgeRest` 8 s); three stalls in a row on the same place within 14 m place the body there when the player cannot see it.
- Stuck on stairs (Loxford Castle): the native pathfinder and RuntimeNavigation allowed a drop sideways off a stair under the floor above; a drop now needs the body's own height clear in the next column (`stepY`/`step`).
- Doors: an open leaf at its outward stop narrowed a doorway and pinned a monk there. A door's leaves are RIGID_NO_MOVERS while anyone but the player is within `doors.passReach` 1.8 m.
- Sunken doorways (marble bunkhouse): ground two above the threshold outside; `doorSteps` now cuts a level landing and a stair up each cell out until the ground is met.
- Ladders: a person more than `ai.ladderAloft` 3.5 m over the ground may take a ladder whatever they are about (a knight stranded in a belfry).
- Carters on ShadowVeil walked for the devmap's caravan stop: `carterRound` (yard <-> local shop counter, `ai.carterLeg` 25 game minutes); the shipment commitment is devmap-only.
- Patrols skip a point whose route search failed.
- Spawn clusters: `AiSystem.scatterStart` begins each census person at their post in working hours, their bed at night, else beside a piece of their workplace or home, `ai.spawnApart` 1.6 m from anyone (`apart()`, `ai.spawnShift` 6); `Offstage.at` spreads the same way. Start clusters 4 -> 0.
- Paintings: canvas layers were 1 mm apart and fought in the depth buffer at a distance; the canvas sits clear of the frame and each layer 3 mm over the last (tapestries likewise).
- Jail: the player's things went to a hidden escrow at y -32 where they were arrested. `Rooms.heldGoods(realm)` (the jail's "Jail's held goods" chest, 320 cells: `Container` chest grids over 100 cells are 16 wide) now holds them (`Container.custodyOf`, saved), locked; release returns them and frees the chest; NPC prisoners keep the hidden escrow.
- Breakout: `escapePlayer` now puts the kingdom's guards and lawmen on a hunt from where the player was (`law.escapeSearch` 120 s), the nearest within `law.escapeHeard` 40 m calls it, and the chat says so.
- Graves: memorial stones had no collision (`Container.stoneSolid`: a static box the size of the slab, made in `Objects.add` for memorials, on burial and on load). Graveyard markers were the style's rock blocks with a BARE_PATCH before them - the "cobblestone patches"; Kit now records them as `oldGraves` and Rooms sets decorative headstones (`Container.decor`: no options).
- Water steps: the chunk mesher drops a liquid top's corner a block where any of its four cells is air over water, and skips the side face onto lower water under open air (key bits 57-60; sloped quads are not merged).
- Inventory: R over an item (pointed at, not only dragged) turns it in place or at the nearest corner of its own space (`InventoryPanel.turnInPlace`).
- World border: `shadowveil.json` `margin` 48: Atlas adds it to every coordinate and `world` = size + 2 margin; Terrain samples every noise at the design's coordinates (`OX`) so the land is cell for cell as before (survey passes; bug-report coordinates are now 48 greater); the coast belt runs round the design's edge and the margin is open sea. `Rpg.worldEdge`: within `world.edgeStart` 40 cells of the world's edge the player takes `world.edgeHurtMin`..`edgeHurtMax` of max health a second. Saves are stamped `worldMargin`; a mismatch carries the character over.
- Fish: one pressed against something `fish.stuck` 0.6 s takes the clearest of twelve headings for `fish.escape` 1.8 s.
- Chandeliers: breakable already; a lit piece now gives no light once wrecked and less as its parts go. `Rpg.restoreWorld` (Wreckage.restoreAll, Breakables.restoreAll with `Breakable` home poses and `rebuild`, Repairs.restoreAll) on load, sleeping (bed and cell), waking from death and resting at a shrine.
- Bodies: "I'll see this to Morrow" -> the kingdom's undertaker by name (`Brain.undertakerName`); dialogue and the death message say the undertaker. A corpse the law takes up is `Corpse.inCare` (saved): it and every stain it leaves are skipped by `Gore.perceive`.
- Foliage pop: any far-chunk eviction bumped `Foliage.epoch` and Gfx let go of every plant mesh; now only evicted chunks' meshes and jobs go. Growth is laid out `foliage.genRings` 4 rings out, `foliage.genPerPass` 3 a pass (was 2 rings, 1 a pass).
- Ragdolls: a body held still off the ground (`Actor.ragCaught`) for `reactions.caughtLimit` 0.9 s is set on the floor under it and got up; the hard limit is `reactions.downLimit` 6 s (was 9).
- Terror: hiding from a dragon went for a hide spot in the open and crouched; now home if near, else `shelterFor` (roofed floor), crouched only under a roof, and running from it when there is none.

### Changes
- Stations: audit probe - 0 of 511 stations without a roof over them (pass 31's removal of `Census.worksites` holds); Gamefixes046Test keeps it so.
- Weapons by tier (HiResItems): Stone knapped (ragged width, flaked shading, no fuller, cord-bound bar), Bronze leaf blade and flared cast guard, Iron forge marks, Black dull edge, White ring over the guard, Steel tapering banded blade with fore-and-aft quillons, Molten glowing veins (armour kept its 0.4.5 per-tier designs).
- Accusations: `Law.charges` (saved `_charges`) records each charge as it is put on a bounty (`chargeOf`: what, to whom, where - the structure's name), the homicide case and the breakout; `halt()` reads them out with the accuser's name.
- Gaol -> Jail in every source, data id and name (loxford_jail etc.).
- E closes the inventory or a container (`Main.eSwallow`: that press does nothing else).
- Weather: engine `Weather.target(name, rain, snow, hail, sand, fog, wind, cloud, storm, drift, dark, tint)` with eased extras and `name()`; lightning by `storm`; `TimeSystem.autoWeather` off. `data/weather.json`: 23 presets (values, hours, group, `natural`, `after`, showers' `pulse`), group-to-group leanings, 5 climates (temperature, swing, weights, dawn fog, afternoon storms), realms/regions -> climate, render settings. `rpg.world.WeatherSystem`: a cycle per climate always running, drawn deterministically, ending on the hour (saved `weatherCycle`), the player's climate applied, cloud drift and shower bursts, temperature; `weather <name>` on the console. Engine `RCMD_PRECIP` (22) + `precip.vert/precip.frag` + `Renderer::precipPass` after TAA: instanced world-space rain streaks along their velocity, flakes, hail, wind-borne sand and drift in a band over the roof, splashes, wrapped round the eye, hidden under `roofTex_`; the old rain pass only when no layers. Gfx: fog tinted and darkened by the weather, the bank closing in with dense fog. Atmosphere: darkness and tint. Brain: shelter from any foul weather. Terrain `regionId`/`regionName`, regions named in data.
- Foley: `audio.json` `footsteps` (feet/hooves loudness, 16 surfaces as layered banks, hoof surfaces, wading, snow cover); new banks step.water/metal/plank; `Sfx.surface` covers mud, gravel, sand, ice, leaves, straw, metal, glass, coal, water; quieter. `Rig.plant` counts each foot set down (`Actor.footPlants`); the player's and NPCs' footfalls follow it while the figure is drawn, the gait clock otherwise. `Animals.hooves` for horses and Pegasi (`audio.hoofHeard` 30).
- Debris: engine `RIGID_YIELD` (1 << 8): in a proxy/yielding contact the proxy's mass is held infinite for the solve (`HeldFast`), so characters push the piece and are never pushed back. Breakables debris and furniture shards use it (were NO_MOVERS).
- Region banner: `HudFragments.RegionBanner` heads `Hud.TOP_STACK` (`hud.regionShow` 5 s, `hud.regionSettle` 1.5 s): realm name or wild region, caption "Kingdom of X - Region" / "The Wilds".
- Shoves: `law.shoveTolerance` 3 within `law.shoveMemory` 120 s; the first two jostle (no knockdown, no crime, no fight).
- NPC poses (Rig State.pray/talk/rest): kneeling with hands together at prayer, a gesturing hand and sway while speaking, rest stances (folded arms, hands behind, hands on hips) changing every 23 s.
