## Pass 35 - Alpha 0.4.4 "Interior Motives" (Hoodie)

The interiors pass on ShadowVeil, with the 0.4.4 bug list, changes and additions. Every enterable building the architecture raised (509 of them, 1,454 rooms) is built inside, furnished, lit and fitted for its work; its people sleep and work in it; every room is reached by the game's own routes from its door; every building is recorded and scored. Each kingdom has a gaol with cells, a bank and a graveyard; ten people were added to keep them. The devmap is untouched (every new behaviour is ShadowVeil-gated, data-only, or a fix to a shared bug).

New tests: `rpg.world.Gamefixes044Test` (every enterable building fitted and approved with no defect, every room lit; every resident owns a bed indoors and their home is it; every resident routed door -> bed and door -> post by RuntimeNavigation; every room of every building routed from its door; each kingdom's gaol cell (locked door), bank branch with a banker, graveyard plot and altar; the ten additions in the world; the version). `Gamefixes042Test`/`Gamefixes043Test` updated to the new rules (honorifics allowed, 215 people, indoor posts checked by 044, version). New probe `rpg.probe.InteriorNavProbeTest` (`-Prpg.probeWho=id,id` maps each storey's cells as the routes reach them; `-Prpg.probeLeg="x,y,z;x,y,z"` routes one leg and prints the nav regions of its ends). Gallery `RPG_GALLERY_ONLY=interiors RPG_MAP=shadowveil RPG_REVIEW_ONLY=all|id,id|purpose:x|kingdom:x [RPG_REVIEW_HOUR=12]`: per building an entrance view, a view of its principal working piece and every room's overview, a labelled contact sheet `sheet-<id>.png`, and the registry (json + md) written beside them with each building's screenshots. `RPG_REVIEW_ONLY=cascade` under p29 renders the tallest fall and a rapid poured. ### The fit-out (rpg.world.shadowveil.Fitout, data/shadowveil_interiors.json)
- Runs in `Architecture.raise` after the registry, on every enterable non-underground entry with interior volumes. Volumes biggest first, ties to the one with fewest doorways through it (a court's gateway range is no nave).
- Storeys read from the cells: a level >= 55% solid with the level over it <= 45% solid is a floor; clear height counted up; under 2 dropped. The old 2x2 wells are filled with the slab's own material.
- Ways in: each doorway's first and second cell inside (kept clear, the step's sides too), and any gap in the volume's wall with open floor two cells beyond it (a tower arch into a nave); a jetty's overhang strip is not a way in.
- Stairs: for each pair of storeys a path of `rise` cells (one step of rise each, the last STAIRS block set in the upper slab), up to two quarter turns, scored by wall contact, distance from doorways and back/front preference (data `stair`: a gaol's at its front), accepted only if both floors stay whole (`whole()`: below, everything the doorways reached still reached; above, everything the stairhead reached with the floor uncut). The slab is opened over steps rise-3..rise-2 (headroom), the steps' bodies filled, a FENCE rail set round the opening only where the floor stays whole. Ladder fallback (LADDER column through the slab) where no flight fits; a ladder storey takes no beds. 477 staircases.
- Rooms: the storey's programme (data `programs`: per purpose `single`/`ground`/`upper`/`top` lists of {use, share, door, walls, axis, minStorey}, or `volumes` in turn) split recursively: walled first (both sides >= 2 deep and `minRoomArea`), within `shareTolerance` of the asked share on the preferred axis then the other, then anywhere; else an open zone; else one room with the other uses merged. Partitions are tried on the grid first (the storey must stay whole through its doorway), run past stair cells, and are built in the wall's inner finish (plaster for timber frame) to the ceiling with a two-high doorway (DOOR or LOCKED_DOOR + InnerDoor when the room asks). A locked room never holds a stair. Unreachable pockets of up to `sealPocket` cells (behind a tower's footing) are walled up flush.
- Furniture: beds first over the whole building (bedroom share, then `sleeps` rooms, then any room not `noBeds`; FOURPOSTER for rich/royal, falling back to BED; nightstand at the head; the flank kept), then each room's pieces (its use's and any merged uses', needs first) by placement: wall/corner (back to a solid non-glass wall; tall pieces never before a window; the front cell kept), centre (table with seats on the long sides: BENCH, CHAIR (+ends), STOOL), counter (across the room, a gap at one end, staff/customer posts behind/before), rows (pews either side of an aisle), altar (far end middle; a `kind` makes it a throne), cells (bars of IRON_BARS with a LOCKED_DOOR gate, a slab, recorded as Architecture.Cell), stalls (fence partitions, hay), trades (the stations the census workers' professions need from census `stations`, else a crafting table), hang/rug/ceiling (no cells). Every solid piece is committed only if every walkable cell stays reachable and every use cell (bed flanks, counter sides, station fronts) too. `fallback` pieces (a RANGE where a FIREPLACE will not fit). Wealth from the programme, size class, or the lot. Every room lit (its pieces, else candles in a corner, else a chandelier).
- Candidate iteration bug found and fixed: `(start + t * stride) % n` revisited a few candidates when stride and n shared a factor; `pick()` now steps by a stride coprime with n.
- Outputs on Architecture: `pieces` (Piece), `innerDoors`, `fittings` (station:K, chest, altar, throne, post:K with use cell), `cells`, `fitted` (per building: rooms (RoomRecord), pieces, stations, fittings, beds, seats, stairs, ladders, walkable/unreached cells, sealed, defects).

### Rendering and performance
- Lights: every point light is buffered and submitted nearest first (`Gfx.pointLight/flushLights`); the renderer keeps only its quality's first N, and ShadowVeil's street lamps alone used to fill it, leaving interiors black by day.
- Interior objects (the SV_INTERIORS band) drawn and lit only within `render.interiorDraw` (30 m). Furniture model keys made once (`Furniture.drawSeed`) and all furniture models prewarmed at load. Stations and chests drawn at their floor (`floorY`); chests drawn with iron straps.
- `Container.solid` places the chest's static body on its floor: it stood at `Ground.at` (the roof for an upstairs chest, the floor above for a downstairs one).
- Objects.update: doors near no one and at rest are not updated (`Door.idle`), doorsForNpcs asks only the people binned within 8-cell squares round each door (it asked every person of 215 for every door); no full list copies a tick. objects.update 2.0 -> 1.0 ms a tick.
- Lamp roof rechecks staggered (`render.lampRechecks` a frame). Foliage chunks meshed on a background thread (`foliage-meshing`), uploads on the render thread, only the chunk underfoot built at once, a 2.5 ms budget (`render.foliageBudgetMs`, `render.foliageReach`). Stutter run: worst 341 -> 60 ms, frames over 33 ms 99 -> 16 (median ~18 ms in the hidden gallery window).
- Shadows (native renderer.cpp/.h): a far cascade kept from an earlier frame is redrawn at once when the slice's middle has moved a tenth of its radius or the sun has moved (`cascadeAt_`, `cascadeSun_`); after turning away from the sun the ground in view fell outside the stale map. Native rebuilt.
- Monsters beyond `monsters.farRest` (220, x+z) from the player rest unsimulated.
- Animals beyond `animals.farSense` (160, x+z) from the player, and not hunting, defending or fleeing, sense once a second instead of five times (animals 7.4 -> 6.3 s over the 3,600-tick ShadowVeil day).
- RuntimeNavigation.corridor retries with the full margin when the tight box finds nothing (a palace's only stair across its court from both ends of a short trip).

### The bug list
- Ripples: `Chemistry.disturb` drops non-finite ripples and clamps radius to (0.05, 8] and strength to 30 (the renderer refuses the whole frame otherwise: a heavy body's fall into water, an electric spell's wide charge); Gfx also guards ripples and water flows (|v| <= 8).
- Idle/spinning NPCs: Order Knights take the next point of their kingdom's patrol round after `ai.knightRoundRest` (40 game minutes) instead of "awaiting a new institutional assignment"; workers with no eligible work tend their workplace (post, its stations and chests in turn); a spin guard in AiSystem.face (a full turn of walking heading within a metre inside `ai.spinWindow` seconds = arrived: hold, re-plan).
- Noclip out of water: `PlayerController.fly` cancels an active swim (the swim's state was never updated while flying and resumed at the old water position).
- Sprint swim pushed under the map: `Swimming.clip` only counts cells ahead of the moving face and never reverses a move (a cell the box already overlapped turned a rise into a drop of the body's height); the swim box only grows where it fits; a box found in rock is eased up.
- Ragdoll into water: `Reactions.afterFrame` hands a knocked-down body lying in water at least `Swimming.FLOAT_DEPTH` deep back at once, where it floats; the swimming takes it.
- Floating objects at 217,39,931 and 459,36,135: square lamp posts (spruce logs) and stall posts were stripped by `clearWays` (plazas count as squares and are not lots); inside a plaza only leaves are cleared now.
- Cows: a domestic beast (not a dog) stands its ground against a person only if hurt by them or its young were (`Animals.youngHurtBy`); else it shies.
- Animals on fences: `Animals.offTheRail` sets a beast standing on a fence, bars or hedge down on the lowest free ground beside it.
- Flowing water: `Terrain.curtains` records every river drop {x, y, z, height, dx, dz} and no longer stacks water blocks; Gfx pours the nearest (`render.fallReach` 48, `render.fallEmitters` 22, `render.fallSprays` 6, `render.fallSheets` 60): gravity droplets (kind 8) over the lip at the river's pace, a translucent curtain with white streaks running down it, foam and mist at the foot.
- Jails: see above. 10 more NPCs: see above.
