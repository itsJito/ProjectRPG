## Pass 38 - Alpha 0.4.7 "Going Underground" (Hoodie)

The 0.4.7 gamefixes pass on ShadowVeil: the bug list (interior foliage, the world's edge and its fog, Store A, shrines on paths, undertakers' bunkers, rails, chairs, tapestries over doors, floating blocks, regional fog and sky cover, rain on foliage, the minimap's wildlife, shrine rest and weather, waterfalls, double doors, animal volume, NPC simulation), the changes (six two-faced interior finishes, chairs and benches, chandelier height and wealth, King Arthur, gear requirements, Ashmaw, banks), the additions (crows, pigeons, vultures, hand-work animations, dungeons under the map, the seven guild halls, the Encounter System) and dialogue names read from the world. Native engine changed (env record 136 bytes with the world edge, bank/fog shaders, chunk mesher's indoor faces and edge clamp, foliage rain): rebuilt with `build.bat game`.

New tests and tools: `rpg.world.Gamefixes047Test` (12 checks: version; no shop called Store A; every guild has a hall with a master; every dungeon level under the ground with open entry, levels below the first, every shaft's ladder whole; lava hurts and Ashmaw has lava; crows, pigeons and vultures fly; six auto-faced interior pairs; chandeliers only with 4 cells of head room; a chair drawn out, sat in, drawn in and pushed home; King Arthur's crown and level; nobody wears what they cannot; every weather bed and its sound, a sconce gives light). `EncounterTest` (6). Probes `DungeonsProbeTest` (levels, entries, walk from mouth to entry, shafts), `BirdsProbeTest`. Gallery `RPG_GALLERY_ONLY=chairs` (`RPG_CHAIR_AT`), `birds`; p16 `RPG_REVIEW_ONLY=handwork` (`RPG_HANDWORK`); p29 `RPG_REVIEW_WHO="Name,..."`; `RPG_REVIEW_IN` now stands the player at the eye (the fog ring is the player's: a body 40 m off hazed every interior review).

### Bugs
- Foliage indoors: `Foliage.roofed()` - nothing grows under a built roof on anything but earth.
- World edge: `Rpg.worldEdge` harms only a swimmer (`world.edgeDepth`). The engine's env record carries the edge (`edge[4]`, `edge_band`); `bank.glsl` `edgeAt/edgeSkyK/bankSkyAt` fog the band `world.edgeFogBand` 64 and the sky over it; the chunk mesher clamps columns past the edge (no void faces).
- Store A: shops on ShadowVeil take their keeper's building's name (`Economy.shadowveilName`); Brain, Dialogue, Quests and Windows read names with `g.nameOf(Ids.X)` and `Rpg.possessive`; `shadowveil.json` `townTalk` per realm replaces the devmap's lines.
- Shrines: `Shrines.onPath`, `shrines.pathClear` 2.
- Undertakers: Architecture "beside" placement, 8 bunkers "later" in the settlements (purpose `bunker`), undertakers' homes the bunker; Grimbold, Odo, Kyrillos, Elidor, Gwenna.
- Fitout: stairwell rail corners joined; chair facing flipped (a chair's facing is its back); `placeHang` checks the wall behind every cell and `doorway()` (no tapestry over a door); chandeliers need `chandelierMinClear` 4 at the very cell they hang over (the storey's tallest was used) and `chandelierWealth` rich/noble/royal.
- Floating blocks: `Terrain.floating()` (26-connected flood from anchored cells; removes what is left; short logs sunk; `RPG_FLOAT_DEBUG`).
- Fog: by region (`realms.*.fogDensity`, `fogTint`, `fogTintBoost` 1.8), sky cover by weather fog (`fog.skyCover`), indoor cut `fog.indoorCut` 0.92 and no regional multiplier under a roof.
- Rain on foliage: `foliage.vert` `uRainFall` droop and patter, wet tint; leaf cards and the g-buffer likewise.
- Shrine rest: `advanceTime` turns the weather over and snaps it.
- Minimap: wild animals (predators orange).
- Waterfalls: `Terrain.curtains` water down drops of 2+; spray emitters (rate <= 200, radius <= 3: the engine rejects more).
- Double doors: half-width leaves meeting at a stile (`Props.doorLeaf(.., paired)`).
- Animal volume: `audio.json` `voices` {volume, reach, gap}, `Sfx.voice`.
- NPC simulation (WorldLifeProbe): locked inner doors had owner 0 and no key, so a banker or chapter head living behind one stood outside all day - `Door.keepers`/`keeps(id)`, every resident and worker of a site holds its locked doors' keys (Census), every `d.owner == n.id` check now `d.keeps(n.id)`; a body pinned in a doorway by an open leaf takes a level step through (`stepToward`, `ai.doorwayReach` 1.6); `AiSystem.apart` checks the whole line to a start cell (the midway cell only let a priest begin outside her dormitory); seat search and chair easing are local (`Objects.near`, `Seating.moving`).
- Suite findings (the full quick suite, run once more at the end of the pass):
  - `Terrain.floating` sank any log 1-2 cells over the ground to the ground: a log lintel over a two-high doorway is exactly that, so the kitchens of log-built lodges and inns (Millwood Lodge, the Chieftain's Hall, Loggers' Rest, the Waystation) were walled shut. Only a lone post sinks now (`heldBeside`).
  - With chandeliers gated by wealth and per-cell head room, plain shops with three-cell ceilings had no light at all: new `Furniture.Kind.SCONCE` (appended; `Furnishings.sconce`, light 6.5 m, drawn from the wall side), tried after candle stands and a fine house's chandelier and before the floor-standing fallbacks; a sconce may hang over a low piece (`lowAt`) and takes down a painting or trophy in its spot (`takeDown`); `interiors.sconcePer` 16.
  - Armourers' forges had no wall left for an anvil and a furnace: `Fitout.ensureTrades` clears a crate, shelves or a dresser from the ground storey (`clearOne`, `MOVABLE`) until the station stands.
  - Loxford Bank two storeys (Hadwin had no bed); Gamefixes041Test reads the bank in `loxford_bank`, Gamefixes045Test counts at least 225 people and 8 outfits.
  - MonsterFightTest's two climb tests (failing since 0.4.6): the engine counted a rung reached only within 0.45 m across while the climb took hold within 0.6 m, so an off-centre climber bobbed on its rung for ever (`ai_service.cpp`: 0.6 m when the waypoint is a rung); an agent with nowhere to go on a ladder holds its rung (`bodies.cpp`: `wishY` 0 holds); a climb is headway (native progress and `Npc.stallPY`); the brain hands the engine the landing past a run of rungs, never a rung in mid-air; no "as close as I can get" on a ladder; `CombatTactics` high ground is never a rung.
- Traps on ShadowVeil: every trap and snare stood at the devmap's floor height (`DevMap.FLOOR`), so none ever sprang; a dungeon's traps lie on their level's `floorY`, a snare on the hunter's ground, and a tread is judged against the trap's own height.

### Optimization (gamefixes: aggressive optimization)
Measured with `gradlew bench`, which now tours ShadowVeil (`Bench.SV_TOUR`: Severia's market and harbour, Loxford by day, by night and in a battle, Millwood, Azharad, Valgard, a sprint) and prints the scene's stretches under `RPG_PROF`; the slow-frame sampler also reports simulation-bound frames.
- `Snapshots.Bodies.mover/ragdoll` and `Agents.agent` by a handle index (each was a search of the list per body per frame: the square of ShadowVeil's bodies).
- Impulsis: a world edit wakes only the bodies about its cells (`VoxelWorld.editsSince`, a ring of 128 edit boxes; the whole world when it overflows or after a clear) - every door that swung woke every body in the world; the broadphase keeps its x order between steps and mends it by insertion; the narrowphase hands the pool only the bodies that touch the world (all 2,874 were jobs); the movers that went are found by binary search. Impulsis 3.4 ms -> 1.9 ms a frame in Severia's market.
- `Voxels` rays and sight lines test only the doors within reach of the segment (`doorsNear`, a 16-cell grid); `Wreckage.ray` only the furniture near the swing (`Objects.near`).
- `Dungeons.update` traps by cell (every trap was tried against every body each frame); `Household` keeps its hearths; `Graveyards` keeps each plot's stones; `Foliage.suitable` looks for a roof only where it could matter.
- Severia's market: simulation 16.0 -> 12.4 ms; the battle 28.8 -> 21.0 ms (whole frame 43 -> 37 ms).
