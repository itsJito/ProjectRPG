### Water (rpg.world.FlowField; engine 2.12)
- The field is grown from the terrain's own water at load (ShadowVeil, ~0.8 s): Dijkstra from open sea and the map's edge, walked upstream (a cell of rise against the flow costs `water.uphillCost` 40), the falls joined (each fall's pool to the first water upstream of it), and every body that never reaches the sea drained toward the far end of its lowest stretch from where higher water meets it (pools fed by nothing stay still). Direction from the neighbours nearer the way out, eased over 5x5; speed `water.riverSpeed` 0.85 for river columns, lakes `water.lakeSpeed` 0.05 rising toward their outflow (`outletReach`), quicker within `lipReach` of a drop (`lipBoost`), slack at the banks (`bankSlack`); whitewater by drops (`foamLip`), plunge pools, and fast water by the banks (`foamFast`). Aqueduct channels overlaid at `aqueductSpeed`. Every river column moves (9,881), 38,934 columns in all. The devmap lays its current boxes into the same field. `Fluids.flow` samples it bilinearly; the old per-current loop is gone.
- Engine: RCMD 21 is now `ASTRATTO_RCMD_WATER_FIELD` (n x n RGBA8: flow x/z at 128 + 32 per m/s, whitewater, open water; corner, cell) kept in a 256x256 texture until replaced; the per-frame rect flows and their uniforms are removed. `water_waves.comp` reads the field; `world_forward.frag` drifts the surface by it at every distance and draws whitewater streaked along the flow and moving with it. Gfx sends a 128 m field at a metre a cell when the eye crosses a 16 m square.
- Falls: the grey box sheet and box streaks are gone; particle kind 11 (falling water): gravity, drawn as a streak along its own velocity (packed into the particle's params like blood's), bursting into a kind 8 spray puff where it lands; ripples pushed into the pool's wave grid (`render.fallStirs`); mist on falls of two or more.

### Optimisation
- Doors: binned once into the movers' 8-cell squares; only doors in squares round anyone moving (and those left open by a passer-by) are looked at - every door did nine boxed lookups twice a tick. Nodes' regrowth once a game minute. Nets and fires kept in a list rebuilt on change (two walks over the whole object list a tick). Containers by kind (`Objects.containers`) - the undertaker scanned every object a tick for graves. Animals: a reused tick list, the pace sent to the engine only on change, the sense timer a field (was a boxed map). Foliage: every chunk's background job queued as soon as it is in reach; finished meshes uploaded within the budget; only a chunk underfoot (`render.foliageUnderfoot` 6 m) with nothing ready is built at once.
- Stutter run (ShadowVeil spawn, 600 frames, walking): before median 18.2 ms, p95 30.4, p99 42.6, worst 59.9, 16 frames > 33 ms; after median 15.7-16.8, p95 25-28.7, p99 31.8-36.7, worst 45-52, 4-12 frames > 33 (run to run). objects.update 746 -> 286 ms over the run, the undertaker off the profile.
